# PDP Copywriting Rules

## Core principles

- Clarity over cleverness.
- Benefits over features, while keeping every benefit within verified feature evidence.
- Specific language over vague hype.
- Customer language over company jargon.
- Active voice over passive voice.
- Honest, supportable copy over sensational claims.
- One main idea per section.

## Style

- Use plain, direct words and short, readable sentences.
- State what the product is before using brand storytelling.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs. Avoid empty terms such as revolutionary, innovative, premium, game-changing, best, perfect, and ultimate unless they are meaningful and supportable.
- Remove filler, unnecessary qualifiers, excessive punctuation, keyword repetition, and generic AI phrasing.
- Match the merchant's demonstrated voice when enough source copy exists; otherwise use a clear, neutral commercial tone.
- Do not imitate distinctive competitor phrasing.

## Product title

- Combine the verified product/category with the most useful differentiator or use case.
- Optimize first for shopper comprehension and click relevance.
- Keep it concise enough to scan and below the runtime contract limit.

## Description

- Open with the clearest value proposition for the intended buyer.
- Explain how verified features support practical benefits.
- Include relevant use, fit, material, formulation, size, care, compatibility, or experience information when supplied.
- Build a logical flow: product and outcome, benefits, proof, usage, objection handling, next step.
- Do not fabricate statistics, testimonials, urgency, scarcity, guarantees, outcomes, certifications, or comparisons.

## Bullets

- Start each bullet with a decision-relevant benefit or outcome.
- Follow with the verified feature or fact that supports it.
- Keep one idea per bullet and avoid repeating the title or other bullets.
- Use 5-7 bullets when enough evidence exists; use fewer rather than padding with invented benefits.

## FAQs and trust copy

- Write questions in customer language and answers in direct, plain language.
- Use FAQs to address actual buyer objections, not to repeat promotional claims.
- Keep unknown answers explicit and identify the merchant information needed.
- Trust copy must name the supporting proof or clearly say that proof is required.

## Quality check

Before returning copy, confirm:

- A cold shopper can identify the product and intended benefit quickly.
- Every factual statement is traceable to merchant evidence.
- No sentence implies certainty beyond the evidence.
- Benefits are specific, non-duplicative, and easy to scan.
- Keywords read naturally.
- Copy contains no unsupported comparative, medical, environmental, safety, financial, or performance claims.
