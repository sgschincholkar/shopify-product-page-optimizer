# PDP SEO Rules

Apply these rules only to the submitted product page. Do not claim a full technical or site-wide SEO audit without crawl, indexation, analytics, or Search Console evidence.

## Search intent and keyword selection

- Infer a primary product query from the product type, buyer need, merchant wording, and repeated language across comparable PDPs.
- Use related terms only when they accurately describe the merchant product.
- Prefer customer language over internal brand jargon.
- Match transactional product-search intent; do not turn the PDP into an informational article.
- Flag uncertain terms instead of presenting guessed search volume or ranking potential as fact.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, repetitive variants, unnatural exact-match phrases, and competitor brand names in merchant copy.

## Product title and H1

- Lead with the clearest product/category phrase when evidence supports it.
- Add the most decision-relevant differentiator, variant, size, material, or audience only when verified.
- Keep the title readable and useful before optimizing length.
- Use one clear H1. Align the product title, H1, and primary search intent without requiring exact duplication.
- Do not add promotional language, unverified superlatives, or strings of near-synonyms.

## Meta title

- Make it unique, descriptive, compelling, and faithful to the page.
- Place the primary term naturally near the beginning.
- Aim for roughly 50-60 characters when possible, but do not damage clarity to hit a character count.
- Put the brand near the end when it fits and is supplied.

## Meta description

- Summarize the verified product value and differentiator in plain language.
- Include the primary term naturally and give a truthful reason to click.
- Aim for roughly 150-160 characters when possible.
- Do not invent discounts, shipping promises, ratings, scarcity, guarantees, or calls to action the merchant cannot honor.

## Description and headings

- State the product and core value early, ideally within the first 100 words.
- Use related language naturally and answer the buyer's likely decision questions.
- Organize content with descriptive heading hierarchy: one H1 followed by useful H2/H3 sections.
- Recommend genuinely useful depth; do not add filler merely to increase word count.
- Avoid duplicate manufacturer copy when original merchant evidence permits a differentiated explanation.

## Images and structured product information

- Recommend descriptive image alt text that explains the visible image; do not stuff keywords.
- Recommend image roles such as product detail, scale, use, comparison, instructions, and proof only when appropriate.
- Note missing product schema inputs such as price, availability, SKU, brand, reviews, and variants, but never claim schema is absent unless rendered markup was actually inspected.
- Never invent structured-data values.

## Boundaries

Do not diagnose crawlability, canonicals, sitemaps, internal linking, Core Web Vitals, international SEO, or indexing from PDP copy alone. Report those only when direct evidence is provided.
