Winnable: one URL in, one upgrade pack out, no store integration needed. Wedge: competitor-benchmarked copy, not generic rewriting. Most teams get wrong: skipping payment and demoing on fake stores; sell to real D2C founders in the room.

How to build it, step by step

1. Post that you are building this, right now: the announcement post is your first impressions and your first signups.  
2. Put a waitlist page live before you build anything: one headline, one email field, hosted on Cloudflare Pages.  
3. Build the smallest sellable loop first: the Hermes browser pulls one real PDP plus 3 competitor pages and outputs title, bullets, FAQ, trust copy, and SEO fields as copy-paste blocks.  
4. Wire the $19 Dodo checkout that unlocks the full pack (free tier shows the title only) before any polish, because real payments take time to arrive.  
5. While the build runs, DM Shopify store owners in D2C founder groups with a free rewritten title for their live PDP and the paid unlock link.  
6. Keep Convex read access to the audits and payments tables open so you can verify every signup and paid audit on demand.  
7. Add store brand-voice memory and the one-click Shopify-field export once the first audit sells.  
8. Collect proof continuously: screenshot each paid audit and any owner who ships the new copy to their live store.  
9. Rehearse the v1 demo: pull a founder's real PDP and 3 rivals, generate the pack, and show the paste-ready result. Payment is deferred to v2.
